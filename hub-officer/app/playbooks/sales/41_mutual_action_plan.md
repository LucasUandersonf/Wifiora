# Block 4 · Joint solution building: the Mutual Action Plan (MAP)

Do not send a proposal and wait. Build the plan **with** the buyer: a reverse schedule anchored on **their** goal date. Closing then stops being "sales pressure" and becomes a mutual operational commitment.

## How to build it
1. **Anchor on the buyer's outcome and date.** "When do you want to see your numbers in one place: before the month-end close? Before the partners' meeting?"
2. **Work backwards** and list every step on both sides, with an owner and a date:
   - "For you to be running on June 1st, we need the contract signed by May 15th, legal approval by May 10th and the technical scope validated by May 2nd. Can we keep this schedule?"
3. **Include the buyer's internal steps** (partner approval, accountant review, data export). Those are where deals stall silently.
4. **Confirm explicitly:** "Does this schedule work for you? What could get in the way?" Adjust together; a plan they edited is a plan they own.
5. **Re-send the MAP after every interaction** with steps checked off. Follow-ups now refer to *their* plan, not to your quota.

## MAP for a self-service SaaS trial (the default for Insighta)
| Day | Owner | Step |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Buyer | Create the account (free trial, no card) |
| 1–2 | Buyer | Import last month's schedule and billing (the first value moment) |
| ~5 | Both | Optional 15-minute review: what the numbers show |
| ~7 | Buyer | Show the dashboard to the decision maker or partner |
| Before the trial ends | Buyer | Decide: choose a plan or close the account. No surprise charges |

Dates are generated by the system from the real trial length. Never invent dates, steps the product does not have, or commitments from the buyer they did not make.

## Rules
- One plan, short, written in the buyer's terms.
- A missed step is a signal, not a reason to push: ask what changed and re-plan or disqualify.
- The goal date belongs to the buyer. If they have none, help them find the cost of waiting (see discovery); do not manufacture urgency.
