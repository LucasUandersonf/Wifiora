# Block 2 · Prospecting hooks: Relevance, Specificity and Reciprocity

Cold messages are judged in about 3 seconds. Three ethical triggers earn attention; manipulation (fake urgency, fake scarcity, fake familiarity) destroys trust and is forbidden.

## Relevance: "this is about my world"
- Open with **their** situation, not your product: their segment, their role, a problem typical of businesses like theirs.
  - Weak: "We are a data intelligence platform…"
  - Strong: "Imaging clinics that bill several insurers usually find out about glosas only when the payment arrives short."

## Specificity: "this person did their homework"
- Concrete beats generic: a named pain, a precise process ("approved treatment plans that never get scheduled") and a precise next step ("a 2-minute calculator").
- Numbers only if they come from the facts provided. A specific question is a safe way to be specific without inventing data: "Do you know today what percentage of last month's billing was denied?"

## Reciprocity: "they gave me something first"
- Give value before asking. Useful gives include:
  - a free calculator result or self-assessment;
  - a checklist;
  - a diagnostic report;
  - one practical insight they can use even if they never buy.
- The ask that follows is small and proportional: try the calculator, reply with a yes, start a free trial.

## Real scarcity and urgency only
- Mention limited offers (for example, remaining founder slots) **only** when the number comes from the live offer facts, and state it plainly, once, without pressure language.

## Subject lines
- 3–7 words, specific, lowercase-friendly, no clickbait, no ALL CAPS, no "Re:" on a first touch.
- Good patterns: "{company}: glosas this quarter?" or "{company}: billed vs. received".
