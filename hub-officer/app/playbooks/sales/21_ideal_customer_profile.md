# Block 2 · Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Spend effort only on companies with **real pain AND financial capacity**. Everything else is noise that burns the sending domain.

## How to define and apply the ICP
1. **Firmographics (capacity):**
   - segment;
   - size proxies: number of units, professionals, reviews, website maturity;
   - region;
   - billing model.
2. **Pain signals (need):** observable evidence that the problem exists and costs money.
3. **Triggers (timing):** new unit, new partners, a hiring spree, complaints about scheduling, recent growth, changes in health-insurance (convênio) rules.
4. **Anti-ICP (disqualify fast):** too small to pay; a closed or inactive business; a franchise unit where decisions are taken at HQ (route to HQ instead); a direct competitor; anyone who opted out.

## ICP for Insighta (clinic data intelligence)
- **Best fit:**
  - private clinics with multiple professionals or units;
  - meaningful health-insurance (convênio) billing: dental, physiotherapy, imaging, labs, medical, ophthalmology;
  - or high-ticket private packages: aesthetics, dentistry.
- **Typical pains:**
  - glosas (insurance denials);
  - no-shows and cancellations;
  - approved treatment plans that never start;
  - revenue that is billed but never reaches the bank;
  - decisions made from spreadsheets.
- **Economic buyer:** owner or managing partner. **Champion:** administrative or financial manager. **Users:** front desk and billing staff.
- **Capacity proxy:** several professionals listed on the site, more than one unit, insurance logos on the site, active scheduling channels.

## Scoring (use to prioritize, not to exclude humans' judgment)
- Score Fit (0–3) + Pain evidence (0–3) + Timing (0–2) + Reachability (0–2).
- Work high scores first. Below 4: do not contact; keep for nurture only if inbound.
