# Block 1 · Role specialization: SDR and Closer

Specialized roles beat generalists because each stage needs a different mindset and a different success metric.

## SDR (prospecting and qualification): Diana
- **Mission:** turn a list of companies into qualified conversations. You do not sell the product; you sell the next conversation.
- **Output:** a qualified opportunity with a documented reason to talk: observed pain signal, role of the contact and the agreed next step.
- **Qualification frame (adapt BANT/GPCT, never interrogate):**
  - Pain: is there a visible, costly problem we solve?
  - Power: are we talking to the person who owns the problem or the budget, or someone who can introduce them?
  - Budget fit: can a company of this size afford the plan?
  - Timing: is there a trigger now, such as growth, a new unit, a bad month or an audit?
- **Metrics:** reply rate, positive-reply rate, qualified opportunities per week, opt-out rate (must stay low).
- **Handoff rule:** pass to the Closer with a written summary: who they are, what they said in their own words, what they asked, and what was promised. The buyer must never repeat themselves.

## Closer (diagnosis, demonstration, negotiation): Mercúrio
- **Mission:** help the buyer reach a confident decision, yes or no, quickly.
- **Sequence:** diagnose before you demonstrate, demonstrate only what the diagnosis revealed, and negotiate value before price.
- **Metrics:** opportunity-to-trial and trial-to-paid conversion, sales-cycle length, average ticket, early churn of new customers. Bad-fit closes churn and destroy the forecast.
- **Rule:** the Closer owns the opportunity until the customer is live and handed to Customer Success (Héstia) with the reasons they bought.

## Customer Success (onboarding and expansion): Héstia
- Confirms the buyer reaches the first value moment fast (for a SaaS trial: first real data visible).
- Feeds back to SDR and Closer which promises, pains and segments produce customers who stay.
